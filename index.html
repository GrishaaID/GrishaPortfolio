How a project usually runs

Three phases, not three services — they overlap in practice, but this is roughly the order a community moves through.

  1. 01

    Set the room up right

    Server structure, roles, permissions, and security bots configured before launch — so the first hundred members land somewhere that already feels intentional, not improvised.

  2. 02

    Hold the line day to day

    Ticket triage, scam and FUD response, AMA hosting, and a moderator team that's actually trained — not just given a role and left alone.

  3. 03

    Grow it on purpose

    Collabs with communities that share a real audience, KOL outreach that isn't spray-and-pray, and weekly numbers the founding team can actually act on.
